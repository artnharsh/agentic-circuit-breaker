# Solar Energy: Fundamentals and Applications

## What Is Solar Energy?

Solar energy is radiant light and heat from the Sun that is harnessed using various technologies
such as photovoltaics (PV), concentrated solar power (CSP), and solar water heating. It is an
abundant, renewable source of energy with enormous potential to meet global electricity demand.

## How Photovoltaic (PV) Panels Work

Photovoltaic cells are made from semiconductor materials, most commonly silicon. When sunlight
(photons) strikes the cell, it knocks electrons loose, generating a flow of direct current (DC)
electricity. An inverter then converts this DC electricity into alternating current (AC) that
can power homes and businesses.

The efficiency of modern commercial silicon solar panels ranges from approximately 15% to 22%.
High-efficiency monocrystalline panels can exceed 22%, while polycrystalline panels typically
achieve 15–18%.

## Solar Irradiance and Capacity Factors

The amount of solar energy reaching Earth's surface is measured as solar irradiance
(W/m²). Average global horizontal irradiance (GHI) varies from around 1,000 kWh/m²/year
in northern Europe to over 2,200 kWh/m²/year in desert regions.

Capacity factors for solar PV range from 10–15% in cloudy northern regions to 25–30%
in sun-rich areas like the Middle East, Australia, and the southwestern United States.

## Solar Power Generation — Global Overview

As of 2023, global installed solar PV capacity exceeded 1.6 terawatts (TW), making it
one of the fastest-growing energy sources worldwide. Solar PV generated approximately
1,600 TWh of electricity globally in 2022.

Key solar-producing countries include China, the United States, India, Germany, and Japan.
China alone accounts for over 35% of global solar capacity.

## Economic Trends

The cost of solar PV has dropped by approximately 90% over the past decade, making it
the cheapest form of new electricity generation in most of the world as of 2023.
The global average levelized cost of energy (LCOE) for utility-scale solar PV fell to
around $0.049/kWh in 2023, compared to $0.375/kWh in 2010.
