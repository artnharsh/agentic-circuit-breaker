# The Solar System: Structure and Celestial Bodies

## Overview

The Solar System is the gravitationally bound system of the Sun and the objects that orbit it. It formed approximately 4.6 billion years ago from the gravitational collapse of a giant interstellar molecular cloud.

## The Central Star: The Sun

The Sun is a G-type main-sequence star (yellow dwarf) at the center of the Solar System. It contains approximately 99.86% of the system's total mass and generates light and heat through the nuclear fusion of hydrogen into helium. Its gravitational field governs the orbits of all planets, dwarf planets, asteroids, and comets.

## The Eight Planets

The Solar System consists of eight recognized planets, ordered by distance from the Sun:

### Terrestrial (Rocky) Inner Planets
1. **Mercury**: The smallest and innermost planet, characterized by a heavily cratered surface, virtually no atmosphere, and extreme temperature fluctuations (-180°C to 430°C).
2. **Venus**: Earth's 'sister planet' in size, but enveloped in a dense, toxic carbon dioxide atmosphere with sulfuric acid clouds, producing a runaway greenhouse effect and surface temperatures around 465°C.
3. **Earth**: The third planet from the Sun and the only known planetary body to harbor life, featuring liquid water oceans, an active nitrogen-oxygen atmosphere, and a protective magnetosphere.
4. **Mars**: The 'Red Planet', possessing a thin carbon dioxide atmosphere, polar ice caps of water and dry ice, ancient dried river valleys, and Olympus Mons, the largest volcano in the Solar System.

### Giant Outer Planets
5. **Jupiter**: The largest planet in the Solar System, a gas giant predominantly composed of hydrogen and helium, featuring the iconic centuries-old storm known as the Great Red Spot and dozens of moons (including Ganymede, Europa, and Io).
6. **Saturn**: The second-largest planet, distinguished by a spectacular and complex system of rings made primarily of water ice and rocky debris.
7. **Uranus**: An ice giant composed of water, ammonia, and methane ices, notable for its extreme axial tilt of approximately 98 degrees, effectively rotating on its side.
8. **Neptune**: The outermost known major planet, a deep-blue ice giant known for active weather systems, supersonic winds exceeding 2,000 km/h, and its large retrograde moon, Triton.

## Asteroids, Comets, and Dwarf Planets

- **Asteroid Belt**: Situated between Mars and Jupiter, containing millions of rocky remnants from the early Solar System, including dwarf planet Ceres.
- **Kuiper Belt & Oort Cloud**: Vast outer reservoirs beyond Neptune composed of icy bodies, comets, and dwarf planets such as Pluto, Eris, Haumea, and Makemake.
